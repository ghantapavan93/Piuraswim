# Source notes

The product catalog snapshot and all image assets used in this concept were retrieved from Piura’s public storefront on September 18, 2026.

- [Home](https://piuraswim.com/) — Eternal Heat, new arrivals, Signature Triangle, Miami Swim Week, Coastlines, waitlist, service language.
- [Story](https://piuraswim.com/story) — founder story, Miami, grandmother, Piura as the City of Eternal Heat.
- [Sunchild Triangle Top](https://piuraswim.com/product/sunchild-triangle-top) — $49 price, S–XL, adjustable ties, true-to-size, fabric and care, pairing.
- [Size Guide](https://piuraswim.com/size-guide) — body measurements, measuring instructions, between-size guidance.
- [Shop](https://piuraswim.com/shop) — 16-piece catalog and Sunchild, Moonchild, Classics families.

Reference research informed mechanisms only: Triangl for independent pair sizing, Monday Swimwear for prominent fit facts, and several other swimwear retailers for fit-guide placement and mobile commerce patterns. Their visual identities, copy, product names, claims, pricing, and proprietary labels were not copied.
